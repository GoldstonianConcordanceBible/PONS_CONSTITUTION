# PONS CONSTITUTION v0.1

Status: PROPOSED FOR GENESIS FREEZE
System: PONS
Governance Layer: 402
Technical Payment Rail: x402 where applicable
Architecture: Human-Governed, Bounded Autonomous Onchain Treasury System

## ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

PONS exists to provide a governed Control Tower for AI-assisted and rules-based treasury management, market observation, trading research, and bounded onchain execution.

Autonomy shall never mean unrestricted authority.

The objective is to reduce unnecessary human intervention while preserving human accountability, deterministic capital controls, reproducible rules, independently verifiable provenance, and the technical ability to stop or contain the system.

## ARTICLE II — GOVERNING DOCTRINE

PONS shall operate according to the following principles:

CONTROL THE CORE.

Critical authority, treasury ownership, governance, and emergency control remain protected.

DISTRIBUTE THE RAILS.

Infrastructure, analytics, data, execution, and public provenance may operate across distributed systems where appropriate.

AUGMENTATION WITHOUT ABDICATION.

AI may observe, analyze, rank, recommend, explain, and propose.

AI shall not receive unrestricted authority over treasury capital, governance policy, signing permissions, or emergency controls.

CAN ≠ MAY.

The technical capability to perform an action does not establish authorization to perform that action.

P&L ≠ SAFETY.

Financial profitability shall never compensate for failure of security, governance, authorization, execution integrity, provenance, or capital containment.

UNKNOWN = DENY.

Unknown assets, contracts, recipients, routers, policy versions, jurisdictions, or execution states shall default to DENY.

FAILURE TO VERIFY = NO TRADE.

If required system state cannot be independently verified, autonomous execution shall not occur.

## ARTICLE IV — 402 GOVERNANCE

402 is the governing compliance, ethics, authorization, audit, and policy layer.

402 determines MAY.

402 responsibilities include:

asset eligibility
wallet authority
contract eligibility
jurisdictional restrictions
capital limits
risk limits
autonomy levels
incident classifications
emergency controls
audit requirements
policy changes
human accountability

402 policy shall ultimately be represented in a machine-readable, signed, versioned form.

## ARTICLE V — x402

x402 is a technical protocol or payment rail that may be used for machine-to-machine transactions where applicable.

x402 does not replace 402 governance.

402 governs.

x402 transports or settles where appropriate.

No transaction becomes authorized merely because it is technically executable through x402 or any other protocol.

## ARTICLE VI — TREASURY

Treasury represents protected ecosystem capital.

Treasury signing authority shall not be available to an AI model, autonomous strategy process, ordinary GitHub workflow, or unrestricted trading bot.

Treasury may allocate bounded capital to approved executor wallets.

Treasury shall remain logically and cryptographically separate from active trading operations wherever practical.

## ARTICLE VII — EXECUTOR

The Executor or Swing Wallet is the bounded operational wallet.

Its authority must be explicitly limited.

The Executor may eventually:

hold approved assets
trade approved pairs
interact with approved contracts
execute approved transaction types
return approved proceeds to Treasury

The Executor shall not autonomously:

change Treasury destination
change owners
add signers
modify governance policy
increase its own authority
interact with unknown contracts
transfer to arbitrary recipients
create unlimited token approvals
deploy arbitrary contracts
bridge capital unless explicitly authorized
disable emergency controls

## ARTICLE VIII — ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI systems, including ENOCH ONE and other models, are advisory intelligence.

AI may:

observe
analyze
score
rank
recommend
generate HOLD / ADD / TRIM / EXIT / RE-ENTER proposals
identify anomalies
produce explanations
assist audits
support research

AI shall not independently modify:

Treasury addresses
wallet owners
signing authority
maximum transaction limits
asset allowlists
contract allowlists
jurisdiction rules
kill switches
emergency state
402 policy

Natural-language output shall never override deterministic governance.

## ARTICLE IX — EXECUTION

Every autonomous transaction shall originate from a canonical Execution Intent.

The intent shall identify, where applicable:

chain
strategy
asset
amount
recipient
router
slippage
deadline
policy version
policy hash

The transaction submitted for signing must correspond to the authorized intent.

Material mismatch between intent and transaction shall result in DENY and incident creation.

## ARTICLE X — FAIL-CLOSED OPERATION

PONS shall default to non-execution when required information is unavailable or contradictory.

Examples include:

unknown asset
unknown contract
stale market data
conflicting required data feeds
policy version mismatch
state-reconciliation failure
unknown recipient
invalid signer state
open critical incident
kill switch active
unverified chain
authorization service failure

The default response shall be:

HOLD
DENY
or ESCALATE

Never best-effort autonomous execution.

## ARTICLE XI — CAPITAL CONTAINMENT

Autonomous authority must remain bounded.

Risk shall not be measured solely by the visible Executor wallet balance.

Effective Agent Blast Radius includes:

wallet balances
active token allowances
session-key permissions
authorized modules
pending transactions
callable contracts
other permissions capable of causing capital movement

PONS shall establish a maximum permitted blast radius for each autonomous Executor.

## ARTICLE XII — HUMAN GOVERNANCE

Human-governed does not mean human-manually-operated.

Humans retain authority over:

constitutional changes
402 policy
capital ceilings
autonomy escalation
new assets
new contracts
exceptional transactions
critical incidents
emergency actions
system restart after serious failure

Routine transactions may later be delegated when they remain completely inside approved deterministic boundaries.

## ARTICLE XIII — PROVENANCE

Every material PONS action should be reconstructable.

The target evidence chain is:

CODE

→ POLICY

→ DATA

→ SIGNAL

→ DECISION

→ AUTHORIZATION

→ EXECUTION INTENT

→ SIGNATURE

→ TRANSACTION

→ BLOCK

→ SETTLEMENT

→ TREASURY IMPACT

→ AUDIT RESULT

Public blockchain explorers may provide convenient evidence but shall not be treated as the entire provenance system.

## ARTICLE XIV — SECURITY

GitHub may store:

code
documentation
schemas
tests
policy templates
audit artifacts
strategy versions
release metadata

GitHub shall not serve as unrestricted Treasury signing authority.

Private keys, recovery seeds, unrestricted signing credentials, and equivalent sensitive authority shall not be committed to source control.

A compromise of a development repository should not be sufficient by itself to compromise Treasury.

## ARTICLE XV — INCIDENT RESPONSE

PONS shall maintain an incident framework including:

SEV-0 — informational
SEV-1 — minor
SEV-2 — material
SEV-3 — critical
SEV-4 — catastrophic

Open SEV-3 or SEV-4 incidents shall prevent autonomous capital escalation and may require immediate autonomy shutdown.

Kill-switch capability must produce actual capital containment rather than merely stopping an application process.

## ARTICLE XVI — RESEARCH AND AUDIT

PONS shall distinguish:

AGENT ASSURANCE

from

STRATEGY PERFORMANCE.

Agent Assurance evaluates:

security
governance
authorization
execution integrity
capital containment
provenance
state reconciliation
incident response

Strategy Performance evaluates:

return
realized profit
unrealized profit
drawdown
benchmark performance
risk-adjusted performance
slippage
turnover
basis risk
market impact

Strong financial performance cannot cure a failed assurance control.

## ARTICLE XVII — EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

PONS research may classify evidence as:

VERIFIED
CORROBORATED
AUTHOR-ORIGINATED
PROPOSED
EXPERIMENTAL
FAILED / FALSIFIED

The system shall preserve failures rather than rewriting history to produce favorable results.

## ARTICLE XVIII — LEGAL BOUNDARY

Technical capability and successful system testing do not independently establish legal classification.

PONS shall not assume that terms including:

educational
utility
token
treasury
autonomous
research
onchain

create exemptions from applicable law.

Legal and jurisdictional determinations relevant to operations shall remain a separate 402 governance workstream and may be translated into machine-enforceable operating restrictions.

## ARTICLE XIX — GENESIS RULE

Before PONS receives meaningful autonomous capital, the system must demonstrate that prohibited transactions are technically constrained rather than merely discouraged by instructions.

The system shall be designed under the assumption that:

AI can be wrong.
Software can fail.
Humans can make mistakes.
Infrastructure can fail.
Credentials can be compromised.
Markets can behave abnormally.

The purpose of PONS governance is to prevent those failures from automatically becoming unrestricted Treasury failures.

## ARTICLE XX — CONSTITUTIONAL STATEMENT

PONS does not seek autonomy without accountability.

PONS seeks bounded autonomy under explicit human authority, deterministic policy, limited capital exposure, independently verifiable execution, and permanent provenance.

THE MACHINE MAY ADVISE.

THE MACHINE MAY EXECUTE WITHIN DELEGATED AUTHORITY.

THE MACHINE MAY NOT DEFINE ITS OWN AUTHORITY.

CAN ≠ MAY.

AUGMENTATION WITHOUT ABDICATION.
